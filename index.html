ReviveHub – AI-Powered Unsold Inventory Marketplace for Startups

A full-stack MERN marketplace that solves a real problem faced by failed or struggling startups — being left with unsold inventory like raw materials, packaging, and finished goods with no way to sell it off. It connects sellers with buyers looking for discounted bulk stock, and with service providers offering screen-printing, packaging, and logistics support to help repurpose leftover goods.

  • AI (OpenAI API) suggests fair resale prices, auto-detects product categories, and semantically matches buyer requirements to listings
  • Real-time buyer–seller negotiation chat via Socket.io before an order is placed
  • Secure Razorpay payment flow with a transparent, formula-based delivery cost calculated from distance and weight
  • B2B surplus-resale marketplace using standard courier-based shipping — not instant/quick-commerce delivery
  • Reviews & trust scores, role-based dashboards for sellers, buyers, and admins
  • "Failed Startup Stories" community feed turning startup failure into shared learning and support
  • JWT-secured authentication throughout
